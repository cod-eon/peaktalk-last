# PeakTalk product model

## Positioning

PeakTalk is a professional AI simulator for preparing difficult work
communications. It is not a generic speech coach.

## Primary user and job

The primary ICP is a Head of Product or senior product leader in a Russian
digital company who must defend a roadmap, budget, priority, or initiative to a
CEO, CFO, or CTO within one to three days.

## First wedge

Defense of an initiative, roadmap, or budget before leadership. The product
cycle is:

```text
material → weak-point discovery → adversarial stress-test
→ text simulation with optional voice input → Defense Brief
```

## Business model and validation

Start with self-serve professional B2C. Corporate L&D is a possible later
channel, not the current enterprise pivot. The first 30-day validation target is
5 target users, 10 paid preparations, 3 users reporting a concrete effect, and 2
users returning or clearly intending to return.

Two discovery contacts are available: an L&D leader at Gazprom-Media (buyer /
expert angle) and an experienced IT project manager and former leader (user /
ICP angle). These are discovery inputs, not validated market evidence.
