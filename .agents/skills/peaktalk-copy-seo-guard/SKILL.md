# PeakTalk Copy SEO Guard

## When To Use

Use for landing copy, scenario pages, metadata, Open Graph, sitemap/robots, SEO pages, ads, email campaigns, onboarding text, paywall copy, and CTA wording.

## Copy Standard

Copy must be concrete and professional. PeakTalk sells preparation for real high-stakes work conversations, not inspiration.

Good angles:

- "AI-стресс-тест аргументов перед рабочей встречей"
- "Проверьте позицию до QBR, защиты бюджета или клиентской эскалации"
- "Получите неудобные вопросы и слабые места аргументации"

Weak angles:

- "Научитесь выступать уверенно"
- "Станьте лучшим спикером"
- "Прокачайте коммуникацию с AI"
- hype-heavy generic AI productivity language

## SEO Rules

- Target scenario-intent queries, not broad public speaking traffic.
- Metadata must match page content and avoid overclaiming.
- Preserve Russian market context unless a task explicitly targets another locale.
- Scenario pages should expose the situation, persona/pressure, and user outcome.
- Do not invent testimonials, statistics, company logos, or guarantees.

## Humanization Pass

Before publishing copy:

1. Remove AI tells: overstructured bullets, inflated claims, "в современном мире", generic transitions, buzzwords.
2. Prefer plain Russian, specific examples, and business context.
3. Keep CTAs action-based: start demo, run stress-test, choose scenario.

## Verification

For SEO changes, check metadata, route rendering, sitemap/robots impact, and obvious duplicate/cannibalized titles.
