# PeakTalk Product Guard

## When To Use

Use when changing product copy, onboarding, scenario pages, guest simulation, pricing/paywall, dashboard information hierarchy, personas, AI feedback framing, or adding/removing features.

## Product Thesis

PeakTalk is a scenario-driven professional tool for stress-testing argumentation before high-stakes work conversations.

It is not:

- a generic public-speaking trainer
- a student presentation coach
- a motivational self-improvement app
- a playful gamified habit product
- a broad "AI communication assistant" without pressure scenarios

## Preferred Language

Emphasize:

- stress-test
- pressure testing
- decision defense
- high-stakes work conversations
- budget defense, QBR, investor pitch, client escalation, stakeholder update
- weak points in argumentation
- preparation before the real meeting

Avoid:

- "speak confidently" as the main promise
- "become charismatic"
- "practice public speaking"
- "unlock achievements", streaks, points, playful rewards
- broad productivity claims without a concrete scenario

## Scope Filter

Before adding a feature, answer:

1. Which real work conversation does this improve?
2. Does it expose or strengthen argumentation under pressure?
3. Is it useful before a real meeting, not just nice to have?
4. Does it fit B2C purchase mechanics without mass-consumer positioning?

If the answer is weak, cut or reframe the feature.

## UX Principle

PeakTalk should reduce preparation ambiguity. It should not bury users in abstract AI options. Prefer concrete scenarios, role pressure, clear next action, and practical feedback.
